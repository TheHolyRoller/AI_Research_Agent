## Must include

Classify each company before compliment or pitch. Use the matching template in `prompts/main.md`: consignment shop, affiliate seller, wholesaler, or closeout buyer. Do not paste one pitch into every row.

Compliments: I like [specific thing they would be proud of] because [concrete reason].

Contacts: on web jobs, if phone and email are both missing, flag `contact_missing` and capture the contact page. LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram do not always need phone or email; owner and compliment still have to be specific.

## Must avoid

Invented owners, emails, phones, quotes, dates, or compliments. Canned icebreakers with no because. M-dashes. One generic consignment line for wholesalers, closeout buyers, or affiliate sellers.

## Success looks like

One row per unique in-scope company or person: class, owner or Not published, proud particular or blank compliment, contacts or a flagged miss plus contact-page URL on web jobs, message built from the class template.
