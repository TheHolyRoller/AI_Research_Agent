# Target Keywords — Dominion Exteriors (dominionext.com)

Keyword research for Dominion Exteriors: roofing, siding, and gutter work in southwest Ohio
(Dayton, Cincinnati, Lebanon, Franklin, and surrounding towns), plus Cleveland and central Ohio
expansion ideas taken from competitor sites.

## How to read this file

- Every metrics table uses the same columns in the same order, and `n/a` is the only null token.
- Metrics come from Semrush exports. Volume is US monthly searches. The export was about one month
  old, and any row with `n/a` for KD or intent had no metric in the export, so it needs a refresh.
- `Intent`: `C` commercial, `T` transactional, `I` informational, `N` navigational. Multiple intents
  are comma-separated, for example `C,I`.
- `KD %`: Semrush keyword difficulty from 0 to 100. Lower is easier to rank.
- `CPC (USD)`: average paid cost per click. `0.00` means no CPC was reported, not that clicks are free.
- `Competition`: Semrush paid competition density from 0.00 to 1.00.
- `SERP Results`: indexed results reported for the query, written as full integers, so `1.4M` is `1400000`.
- `<domain> Position`: that domain's organic position exactly as exported. `0` means the export recorded
  no position for that domain.
- Competitor tables are sorted by volume (high to low), then KD (low to high).
- Plain keyword lists carry no metrics. They are idea and queue lists, deduplicated, in original order.
